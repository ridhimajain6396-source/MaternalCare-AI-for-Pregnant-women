# MaternalCare India

**MaternalCare India** is a bilingual maternal health and wellness platform designed to support pregnant women, post-delivery mothers, and frontline health workers with digital guidance, risk awareness, and care tracking.

The platform follows a government-style healthcare service model and provides information in **English** and **Hindi**.

---

## Key Modules

- **Beneficiary Registration**  
  Records maternal profile, pregnancy stage, BMI, expected delivery date, and basic medical details.

- **Maternal Journey Guide**  
  Provides trimester-wise pregnancy guidance and progress tracking.

- **Baby Development**  
  Shows week-by-week baby growth details and month-wise development videos in English/Hindi.

- **Health Tracker**  
  Tracks BP, hemoglobin, blood sugar, symptoms, water intake, sleep, mood, and other health indicators.

- **AI Risk Assessment**  
  Uses a machine learning model to classify pregnancy risk as low, medium, or high.

- **Medicine & Nutrition Support**  
  Helps monitor supplement compliance and daily nutrition quality.

- **Yoga & Wellness**  
  Provides safe yoga, meditation, and breathing guidance.

- **Government Benefits**  
  Offers eligibility guidance for maternal and child health schemes such as JSY, PMMVY, JSSK, Poshan Abhiyaan, and Ayushman Bharat.

- **Post-Delivery Care**  
  Supports postnatal recovery, breastfeeding review, newborn care, and postpartum depression screening.

- **Kick Counter**  
  Helps monitor fetal movement during late pregnancy.

- **AI Maternal Health Chatbot**  
  Provides basic guidance on diet, checkups, medicines, warning signs, breastfeeding, PPD, and schemes.

- **Emergency Guide**  
  Lists critical pregnancy and post-delivery danger signs with emergency helpline guidance.

- **Dashboard & Analytics**  
  Displays beneficiary data, health records, risk distribution, and summary charts.

## Safety Note

This platform provides general maternal health guidance and digital support. It does not replace professional medical consultation.

For emergency symptoms such as heavy bleeding, severe headache, blurred vision, convulsions, high fever, difficulty breathing, chest pain, or reduced baby movement, contact emergency services immediately.

**Emergency Helpline: 108**

---

## Purpose

The objective of MaternalCare India is to improve maternal health awareness, support early risk identification, encourage regular checkups, and provide accessible digital guidance for pregnancy and postnatal care.
